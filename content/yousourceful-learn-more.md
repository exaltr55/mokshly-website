## 4 — Understand: the 5S Framework

- **Eyebrow:** Understand — the 5S Framework
- **Headline:** Five lenses on a single experience.
- **Body:** The 5S is a simple way to see what's already happening inside any experience — not five steps to follow, but five dimensions of one moment. The same lenses fit a hard conversation, a new opportunity, working alongside AI, or an ordinary Tuesday.
- **The five (cards):**
  - **Source** — Awareness, the space in which experience arises.
  - **Seed** — The patterns created through our thoughts.
  - **Soil** — The resources available within us and around us.
  - **Season** — The conditions, context, and phase an experience unfolds through.
  - **Sowing** — Our intentional participation in what happens next.

---

## 5 — Practice: the Seven Practices

- **Eyebrow:** Practice — the Seven Practices
- **Headline:** Closing the distance is a practice.
- **Body:** Understanding is a beginning, not an arrival. There's real distance between knowing a better way to respond and actually finding it — in the meeting, in the disagreement, at the moment it would have mattered. Closing that distance is a practice.
- **Body:** You already breathe, think, talk, write, move, and rest. Each practice turns one of these into a quiet way back to yourself — a minute or two at a time.
- **The seven (cards):**
  - **I AM Breathing** — Returning through the breath.
  - **I AM Thinking** — Meeting thought with a simple anchor.
  - **I AM Talking** — Awareness while speaking and listening.
  - **I AM Writing** — Seeing your thinking on the page.
  - **I AM Moving** — Coming back through the body.
  - **I AM Resetting** — Creating space from the constant stream.
  - **I AM Aligning** — Bringing life gently back into balance.
- **Closing line:** Practices are invitations, not prescriptions — adaptable to you and the moment. There's no score to chase and no need to catch up.
